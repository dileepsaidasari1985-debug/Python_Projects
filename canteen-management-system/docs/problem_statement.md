# Problem Statement

## Case Study #57: Smart Canteen Order and Stock Management

Campus canteens serve hundreds of students during peak breaks. When orders are taken sequentially without real-time inventory checks, staff frequently oversell depleted snacks, miscalculate bills, and lose visibility into remaining stock and daily sales totals.

**Problem Statement:**
Design and implement a **Smart Canteen Order and Stock Management System** in Python that processes successive customer orders, calculates itemized bills with campus GST, updates available SQLite inventory atomically per valid line item, rejects unavailable or invalid quantities with clear explanations, and summarizes daily sales and remaining stock with ASCII gauges and CSV exports.
